Privacy

Everything you paste or type into a calculator is processed in your browser and never sent anywhere. The site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and reports only aggregated, anonymous numbers. Apart from that beacon, fonts and scripts are served from this site itself, so no other third party sees your visit. Your light or dark theme choice is kept in your browser’s local storage and never leaves it.

Your pasted metrics and results never leave the browser.
